CRM, Data & Process Improvement
Solving problems through simplicity

My professional journey began with research and data. I was interested in understanding information, identifying patterns, and finding what was missing.

Over time, this led me into CRM systems, workflow improvement, and automation. I often found that data issues were not only about the data itself, but about how processes were structured, how information moved between systems, and how teams worked with it in daily practice.

In my work, I focus on understanding where processes break, improving data reliability, and creating solutions that reduce manual work and support better everyday operations. My background in Social Psychology helps me understand both systems and people. I enjoy bringing structure to complexity and turning scattered information into practical and useful solutions.

How I work

  • I start by understanding how processes work in practice and where problems appear in daily work. These are often related to manual steps, missing information, or inconsistencies between systems.
  • From there, I identify realistic needs and translate them into clear workflows that are practical, useful, and sustainable for the teams using them.
  • I use automation where it helps reduce repetitive work and improve consistency. At the same time, I focus on making sure that the data remains accurate, reliable, and useful for both daily operations and reporting..
  • I work closely with stakeholders and developers to define needs, test solutions, and make sure that what is built supports actual business processes.
  • Good solutions also depend on how people use them. For this reason, I support teams in understanding the tools, using data correctly, and working with systems with more confidence and independence.
  • I continue to develop my skills because there is always something to improve, simplify, or understand better.
