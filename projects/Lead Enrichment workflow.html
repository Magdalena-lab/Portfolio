Lead Enrichment Automation

This project is a low-code automation pipeline built in Zapier that triggers the moment a lead submits a website form. It extracts the company domain from their email, passes it to OpenAI GPT-4for deep enrichment, parses the structured JSON response with a JavaScript code step, and writes a lead score, summary, pain points, lifecycle stage, and personalised outreach opener directly into HubSpot CRM. Lastly creates a follow-up task and send notification to the sales representative Gmail. No manual steps needed.

Process Prototype

Lead intake enrichment and routing workflow designed for CRM operations

Context

Exploring a more structured lead intake process

I built this project to explore how lead intake could be handled in a more structured way before the first sales interaction. In many teams, a new form submission often requires manual research before anyone can qualify or prioritise the lead. Company context, likely needs, and follow-up actions can depend on whoever picks up the lead first, which can slow response time and create inconsistent CRM data.

Approach

I designed a 7-step enrichment and routing workflow

I built this workflow in Zapier with HubSpot as the destination system. It starts when a form is submitted. The workflow extracts the company domain from the contact email, enriches the lead with company context, generates a lead score and maps the output into CRM fields. The enriched record is then written to HubSpot, a follow-up task is created, and the sales team receives a notification with the lead context ready for first contact.

Outcome

Lead context prepared in under 30 seconds

From form submission to an enriched HubSpot record, the workflow completes in under 30 seconds. The project shows how enrichment, qualification support, and task creation can happen automatically at the point of lead capture. Instead of starting with manual research, sales receives a lead record with structured context already in place.
