HubSpot Workflow Automation

This project demonstrates my job search workflow automation in HubSpot, tracking applications, follow-ups, and outcomes efficiently.

Workflow Objective: Automatically track applications with no response.

Logic: Deals in the Application stage with no activity for 14 days are automatically moved to No Response.

Purpose: Prevents opportunities from stagnating and helps prioritize follow-ups.

Insights:

  • Track application status efficiently
  • Analyze patterns of invitations, rejections, and offers
  • Maintain structured records for personal tracking and reporting
